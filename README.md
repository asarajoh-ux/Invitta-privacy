Invitta — Save Smarter. Reach Every Goal.
Invitta is the AI-powered savings goal planner that reveals hidden costs, tracks your progress, and coaches you to the finish line.

That $40k car? It's really $79k after insurance, taxes, maintenance, and more. Invitta automatically uncovers the costs most people forget — so you know the true price of every goal before you start.

Features
AI Coach — Ask anything. Your AI Coach sees your goals and gives exact numbers: what to prioritize, whether you can afford two goals at once, and how to save smarter.
Hidden Cost Calculator — Automatically fills in taxes, insurance, maintenance, fees, and more for each goal type, so you see the full picture.
Progress Tracking — Beautiful visual dashboards show how much you've saved, how far you've come, and what's left.
Recurring Contributions — Set up weekly, biweekly, or monthly auto-savings schedules to stay on track without thinking about it.
Growth Projections — See your projected growth over time with compounding returns. Adjust your savings rate and watch your timeline shift in real time.
Goal Snapshots — Save and restore your entire goal portfolio. Compare scenarios and never lose a configuration.
Goal Types
House, Car, E-Bike, Boat, College, Horse, Wedding, Apartment, Debt Payoff, Emergency Fund, Retirement, Business, Vacation, and Custom goals.

How It Works
Choose a Goal — Pick from 14 goal types or create your own. Enter your target amount and timeline.
