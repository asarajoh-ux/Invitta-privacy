Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new savings goal?
Open the app and tap the Plan tab, then tap the add button. Choose a goal type (house, car, vacation, etc.), enter your target amount and date, and the app will calculate how much you need to save each month.

How do I log a contribution toward a goal?
Tap on any goal from your Dashboard, then tap the add contribution button. Enter the amount and optionally add a note. Your progress will update automatically.

What is the AI Coach?
The AI Coach analyzes your goals and provides personalized guidance on prioritization, affordability, savings strategies, and hidden costs you might be missing. It is available on the Coach tab.

How do I set up recurring contributions?
Open a goal, go to the funding section, and tap to create a recurring schedule. Choose weekly, biweekly, or monthly frequency and the amount you want to save automatically.

How do I restore a subscription?
Go to the subscription screen and tap "Restore Purchases." Your previous subscription will be detected and reactivated if it is still active.

How do I delete a goal?
Open the goal you want to remove, then tap the delete option in the goal settings. This removes the goal and all its contributions.

Is my data secure?
Your data is stored securely with encrypted connections and protected by Row Level Security. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Does Invitta give financial advice?
No. Invitta is a planning and tracking tool that provides general guidance. It is not a financial advisor. Always consult a qualified financial professional before making financial decisions.
