
<h1>Invitta Privacy Policy</h1>
<p class="updated">Last updated: October 13, 2026</p>

<p>This Privacy Policy describes how Invitta ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Invitta mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.</p>

<h2>1. Information We Collect</h2>

<p><strong>Information you provide:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Financial goals:</strong> Goal titles, types (e.g., house, car, vacation, retirement), target amounts, current savings amounts, target dates, and related financial details you enter (such as property price, down payment percentage, loan terms, and cost estimates).</li>
  <li><strong>Contributions:</strong> The amounts and dates of contributions you log toward your goals, along with any optional notes you add.</li>
  <li><strong>Recurring schedules:</strong> Automated saving schedules you set up, including frequency and amounts.</li>
  <li><strong>AI Coach messages:</strong> The questions and messages you send to the in-app AI Coach, along with the responses generated.</li>
  <li><strong>Disclaimer acceptances:</strong> A record that you accepted the app's disclaimer, stored with a locally generated device identifier.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Information collected automatically:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Device identifier:</strong> A randomly generated ID stored locally on your device (in browser/device storage). This is used to associate your data with your device. It is not tied to your personal identity and is not shared with advertisers.</li>
  <li><strong>Camera access (optional):</strong> If you choose to scan a receipt, the app requests camera access. Camera input is processed on-device and is not stored or transmitted unless you explicitly save the resulting data as a contribution.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Information we do <em>not</em> collect:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>We do not require an account, email address, or password to use the App.</li>
  <li>We do not collect your name, phone number, or physical address.</li>
  <li>We do not track your location.</li>
  <li>We do not sell or rent your data to third parties.</li>
</ul>

<h2>2. How We Use Your Information</h2>
<ul>
  <li>To store and display your savings goals, contributions, and progress.</li>
  <li>To power the AI Coach feature, which analyzes your goals and provides personalized financial guidance.</li>
  <li>To manage recurring contribution schedules.</li>
  <li>To record your acceptance of the app's disclaimer for legal compliance.</li>
  <li>To verify and manage premium subscriptions and entitlements.</li>
</ul>

<h2>3. Third-Party Services</h2>

<p>The App uses the following third-party services to function. Each has its own privacy policy:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Bolt Database</strong> — Hosts the database where your goal data, contributions, chat messages, and disclaimer acceptances are stored. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. See <a href="https://supabase.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener">supabase.com/privacy</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Qonversion</strong> — Manages in-app purchases and subscription entitlements. Qonversion processes transaction data to verify purchases and manage your subscription. See <a href="https://qonversion.io/privacy-policy" target="_blank" rel="noopener">qonversion.io/privacy-policy</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Apple App Store / Google Play</strong> — Process in-app purchases. Your payment information is handled by Apple or Google, not by Invitta. We do not see or store your credit card details.</li>
  <li><strong>AI API providers</strong> — When you use the AI Coach, your messages and goal summaries are sent to an AI service to generate responses. No data is retained by the AI provider beyond what is necessary to process the request.</li>
</ul>

<h2>4. Data Storage and Security</h2>
<p>Your data is stored in a Bolt Database protected by Row Level Security policies. Data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections. While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.</p>

<h2>5. Data Retention</h2>
<p>Your data is retained for as long as you use the App. You can delete individual goals and contributions at any time within the app. If you wish to delete all your data, you can delete your goals one by one, or contact us at the email below and we will remove your data within 30 days.</p>

<h2>6. Children's Privacy</h2>
<p>The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.</p>

<h2>7. Your Rights</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Access:</strong> You can view all your data within the App at any time.</li>
  <li><strong>Deletion:</strong> You can delete goals, contributions, and chat history within the App. You may also request full deletion by contacting us.</li>
  <li><strong>Opt-out:</strong> You can stop using the App at any time. Uninstalling the App removes the local device identifier from your device.</li>
</ul>

<h2>8. Disclaimer</h2>
<p>Invitta is a financial planning tool that provides general information and guidance. It is not a financial advisor, and its content should not be considered financial advice. Always consult a qualified financial professional before making financial decisions.</p>

<h2>9. Changes to This Privacy Policy</h2>
<p>We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the App and on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.</p>
